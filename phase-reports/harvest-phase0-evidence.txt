CONCLUSION — variance has TWO faces around a ~410–650 render-depth jitter:
  (a) EXPANSION between runs ⇒ phantom "new" deltas (false alerts);
  (b) DROPOUT between runs ⇒ baseline silently shrinks (totals fluctuate,
      historical IDs vanish from the "current" view).

DESIGN REFINEMENT for Phase 2 (important):
  A seen-ID union ALONE is insufficient. A deeper render legitimately
  contains IDs never seen before (they'd fire once through the union too).
  The discriminator for "newly POSTED" vs "newly DISCOVERED" is recency:
  harvested reviews carry relative_date. Rule: new ID + recent date
  (≤ N days, N≈30) ⇒ posted-new (alert); new ID + old/unparseable date
  ⇒ backfill discovery (merge into union silently, count separately).
  First-run flood: seed union from the first harvest, suppress/mark its
  delta as "baseline", never alert.
